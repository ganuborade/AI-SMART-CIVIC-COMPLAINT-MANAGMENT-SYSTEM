THE PROBLEM • CURRENT MUNICIPAL BOTTLENECK

Why Traditional Civic Complaint Systems Fail

Municipal corporations manage millions of residents, yet still rely on manual triage and fragmented communication.

⏱️
Slow Manual Triage
Clerks take 24 to 72 hours just to read and forward complaints to appropriate ward engineers.
🔀
Misrouted Tickets
Over 60% of waterlogged street issues get shuffled back and forth between Road and Drainage departments.
👻
'Ghost' Resolutions
Officers mark complaints 'RESOLVED' in software without any physical proof or site inspection.
📉
Zero Accountability
Citizens have no transparency, cannot verify repair work, and cannot provide supervisor feedback.
THE INNOVATION • SMART GOVERNANCE

Our Solution: Closed-Loop AI Governance

An end-to-end digital ecosystem transforming how citizens and municipal authorities interact.

📱
1. Citizen Geo-Report
Citizen snaps a photo of the defect (pothole, water leak, fallen line). Automatic GPS pinpoints the exact latitude and longitude.
⚡
2. Instant AI Triage
AI analyzes description and image tags, detects category (e.g. WATER_LEAKAGE), calculates hazard severity (CRITICAL), and auto-assigns the target department.
📸
3. Verified Resolution
Field engineer receives work order on their mobile/web portal, repairs the issue, and uploads mandatory AFTER photo proof for citizen review.
